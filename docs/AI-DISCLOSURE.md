## What the AI did and did not do

- **Design:** Claude and Codex produced architectures independently from the same brief. [ARCHITECTURE-DECISIONS.md](ARCHITECTURE-DECISIONS.md) records what was taken from each.
- **Code:** written by Claude. The decision policy is deterministic code. **CAVEAT itself calls no language model at runtime**, and no answer in the demo is generated by a model.
- **Reviews:** Codex found 9 defects in review 1, 3 in review 2, 3 in review 3 and 2 in review 4. Each fix has a regression test. Of the review-1 regressions, 7 of 9 fail on the pre-fix code; the unit-level regressions for reviews 2 and 3 (R2-2, R2-3, R3-2, R4-2) all fail on the pre-fix source, as does R5-1, a defect Claude found after review 4. The fixes for review 4 have not been reviewed by Codex again.
- **Data:** every person, place and record in the demo is fictional.
- **Human review:** none is claimed. No human has listened to the narration or reviewed the code as part of this build, unless the owner records it here.
